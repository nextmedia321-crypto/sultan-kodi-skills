# Capital gains tax helper

1. Ask for the tax country and year; rules differ and change, so check an official source for the current rates and say which one you used.
2. Collect each sale: asset, buy date and price, sell date and price, fees, currency.
3. Compute gain or loss per lot and totals, applying the holding-period and loss-offset rules from the source, and show the formula.
4. Produce a clean table the user or their accountant can use.
5. Say plainly that this is an estimate and a tax professional should confirm it.
