# Distinctive landing page design

Before coding, decide in writing: audience, the one action the page drives, and a visual direction (e.g. editorial, brutalist, warm and human, technical). Avoid the default look: centered hero with gradient blob, three identical cards, stock icons.

## Craft rules
- Type: pick two fonts at most, strong scale contrast, real line-height. Support Hebrew with a proper RTL font when needed.
- Color: one dominant, one accent, neutral base. Check contrast at 4.5:1.
- Layout: break the grid on purpose once or twice; use whitespace as a design element.
- Content first: real headline that states the outcome, proof (numbers, logos, quotes) near the top, one clear call to action repeated.
- Motion: small and purposeful; respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Responsive from 360px up.

## Process
Sketch structure as a list of sections, then build, then screenshot at mobile and desktop and critique your own result honestly before showing it. When redesigning, keep the copy and data, change the system.
