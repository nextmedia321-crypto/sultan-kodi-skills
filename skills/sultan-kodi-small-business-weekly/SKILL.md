# Small business weekly brief

1. Gather what is available (connected apps, spreadsheets, pasted numbers). Say which data you could not get.
2. Report: cash in and out, sales versus last week, new leads and response time, unpaid invoices by age, upcoming commitments.
3. Finish with the three most valuable actions for the week and why.
4. One page, plain language, numbers with dates. Never fill gaps with guesses.
