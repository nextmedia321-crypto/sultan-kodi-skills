# Market news brief

1. Get the watchlist; search recent news and company announcements.
2. For each item: headline, source, date, one-sentence fact, why it might matter, and whether it is confirmed or rumor.
3. Group by company, then macro. Drop duplicates and clickbait.
4. End with scheduled events in the next days (earnings, rate decisions).
Decision-support only: not financial advice. State assumptions, show sources and dates for data, and never execute trades.
