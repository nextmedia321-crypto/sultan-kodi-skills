# Investment budget

1. Gather: monthly net income, fixed expenses, debts, emergency fund months, goals and horizon.
2. Recommend a structure: emergency fund first, then goal buckets, then risk capital with a hard cap (as a percent of net worth).
3. Show three allocation scenarios (cautious, balanced, growth) with the reasoning and a worst-case drop in currency terms.
4. Keep a simple monthly plan table the user can edit.
Decision-support only: not financial advice. State assumptions, show sources and dates for data, and never execute trades.
