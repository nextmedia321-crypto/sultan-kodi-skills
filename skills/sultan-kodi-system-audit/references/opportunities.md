# Ranking opportunities

Each opportunity gets: what, why, who benefits, evidence, then three scores from 1 to 5:
- Impact on the goal it serves: revenue (conversion, average order, price, upsell), retention (activation, habit, support load, trust), or risk (security, legal, outage, data loss).
- Confidence in the impact (evidence: analytics, user complaints, comparables, a defect found).
- Effort (1 small, 5 large).
Priority = impact x confidence / effort. Show the top ten as a table with the goal tag (revenue, retention or risk) and a one-line first step. Risk items that are Critical findings go to the findings ledger, not here. State plainly when impact is a judgment without data. Proposals only: build on request.
