# Politician trades

Source: official periodic transaction reports published by the legislature's own disclosure office (for the US House, the Clerk's site; the Senate publishes separately). Use only official or clearly attributed sources.

## Procedure
1. Identify the member and the time range.
2. List new filings; for each trade capture: owner (member, spouse, dependent), asset and ticker, buy or sell (and options strike and expiry if any), transaction date, filing date, **days to disclose**, and the amount **range** (disclosures give ranges, not exact values).
3. Flag trades in stocks the user holds or watches.
4. Add context: committee roles, pending legislation or events near the trade date, as facts only.
5. Alert once per new filing; do not repeat.

## Always say
Disclosures are delayed (up to about 45 days), amounts are ranges, trades by family may not be the member's decision, and a trade is not evidence of wrongdoing or a reason to copy it.

**Disclaimer (include in every result):** this is decision support and information, not investment, tax or legal advice. Data can be delayed or wrong; verify with the original source. It never places orders or connects to a broker.
