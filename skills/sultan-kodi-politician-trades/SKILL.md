# Politician trades

1. Use official disclosure databases and state the disclosure and transaction dates and the amount ranges (disclosures give ranges, not exact sizes).
2. Summarize by person, ticker and direction, and highlight patterns such as clustering or trades near relevant legislation, labeled as observations, not accusations.
3. Note the reporting delay and that disclosure does not imply wrongdoing.
Decision-support only: not financial advice. State assumptions, show sources and dates for data, and never execute trades.
